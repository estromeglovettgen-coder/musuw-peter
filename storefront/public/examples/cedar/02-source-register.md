# Cedar source register — evidence rules

Demo project / 演示项目。Original fictional material for a product walkthrough, not real customer data.

## Required record for each external claim

Record the source title, source date, the exact passage used, and the limited claim that the passage can support. Include qualifications such as scope, conditions, and uncertainty. A source entry is incomplete if its date or relevant passage is missing.

## Evidence review before publication

A reader should be able to identify the source behind a claim and inspect the relevant context. A reference alone does not prove that an interpretation is correct. Do not convert a proposal or assumption into a verified finding.

If sources conflict, show the disagreement and check whether a later review supersedes an earlier plan. If the available sources do not answer a question, record it as unknown rather than inventing the missing detail.

## 中文摘要

每个外部主张必须登记来源标题、日期、使用的原文段落及其能够支持的有限结论，并保留适用条件。不把方案或假设写成验证结果。遇到冲突须检查版本；没有资料的内容应标注未知。
